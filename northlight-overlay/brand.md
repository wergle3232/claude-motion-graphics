# Brand: Northlight

Written 2026-10-08 from answers in chat (a made-up brand for the plugin's trial pieces). Claude reads this file before every piece. Change it by hand or ask Claude to; the colour and font tables are read by a script, so keep their layout.

Northlight is a weekly video podcast: long conversations with people building energy and climate companies.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #0D1117 | plates behind text over footage |
| Surface | #161B22 | cards |
| Text | #F5F7FA | all text |
| Muted text | #A3ADBA | roles, captions |
| Accent | #FFC857 | warm "north light" yellow: lines, the one highlight |
| Second accent | #5EEAD4 | teal: data, second highlight |
| Border | #2A313C | hairlines |

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weights | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | Bricolage Grotesque | 700, 800 | Google Fonts |
| Body | Manrope | 500, 700 | Google Fonts |
| Numbers and labels | JetBrains Mono | 500 | Google Fonts |

## Logo

- No logo file: the wordmark is "NORTHLIGHT" in JetBrains Mono, wide letter spacing, with a small yellow square before it.

## Look

Editorial and confident over real footage. Dark plates with a soft blur behind text so it reads over any shot; one yellow line or mark per graphic. Text anchored to the lower-left and upper-right thirds, never over the guest's face. Motion is quick in, still while it reads, quick out.

## Words

Short and specific. Names and roles exactly as the guest gives them. Quotes in the guest's own words, never paraphrased.

## Avoid

Graphics over the centre of the frame (the face), neon, gradients, emoji, more than one graphic on screen at a time.

## Sound

The overlays are silent: the conversation is the sound.
