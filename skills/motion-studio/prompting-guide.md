# Prompting guide: say this, get this

You do not need long prompts. Say what the piece is for and what should happen, in your own words, then steer from the first version. Each line below is a good first sentence for a kind of piece, with a finished example made the same way ([examples/](examples/)). The examples were each made over a short conversation (a first version, then a few notes), not from one sentence.

## First sentences

| You want | Say something like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A title card for your video | "Make a 5-second title card for my video: *Motion graphics*, made with Claude Code." | [ex-titles](examples/ex-titles/) |
| To explain a process quickly | "Show *Idea, product, result* as an animated diagram made of simple shapes." | [ex-shapes](examples/ex-shapes/) |
| A stat for a post or a video | "Make the number $2.95 land hard, with a label." | [ex-data](examples/ex-data/) |
| A product feature promo | "Show this screen of my app, zoom in on the export button." (attach a screenshot) | [ex-screen](examples/ex-screen/) |
| Graphics over your own footage | "Add a name bar and the key numbers over my clip, each on the word I say." (give the clip) | [ex-overlay](examples/ex-overlay/) |
| An intro or outro | "Make a 3-second logo sting from my logo." | [ex-logo](examples/ex-logo/) |
| To show how something moves | "Make a ball drop in real time, then the same drop in slow motion with labels." | [ex-ball](examples/ex-ball/) |
| A promo for Reels and LinkedIn | "Make a 15-second promo for our booking feature, for Reels and LinkedIn. End with our website." | [kiln-promo](examples/kiln-promo/) |
| A carousel | "Make a 6-slide LinkedIn carousel explaining our three pricing plans." | |

Your brand (colours, fonts, logo, words) comes from `brand.md`, made once from your website: "Set up my brand from https://your-site.com".

## Worth saying when it matters

- **Where it goes:** "for Instagram Reels", "for my YouTube video", "to lay over my footage in Resolve". That decides the size and whether it needs a see-through version.
- **The exact words** if they matter ("the headline is *Book in two taps*"). Otherwise Claude drafts them.
- **Real material:** screenshots, your logo files, a clip, the numbers and where they come from. Real beats drawn.
- **A reference:** "like this video" (a link or a file), or "like the ball one we made, but raining balls". Claude copies the feel, never the content.
- **Sound:** "no music", "soft clicks", or the music file you chose.
- **"Storyboard first"** if you want to see still frames before anything is built (longer or important pieces).

## Steering a version

Say what to change and where, in plain words. The moment in seconds helps.

- "At 4.5 s the logo comes in too late."
- "Slow it down a little."
- "The text is all in the middle; bring some in from the top."
- "It's jerky when it zooms in on the guitar player."
- "Make the drop faster."
- "That's it, make it final."
