# Example: Screens (MORF, 16:9, 9 s)

**The job:** A product feature promo: a screen tilted in 3D, zoomed to one part, with a callout.

**A sentence to start one like it:** "Show this screen of my app tilted in 3D, zoom in on the export button and point at it with a short callout."

It was made over a short conversation, not from that one sentence: a first version, then a few notes. The sentence is a good way to start.

**What is on screen**

- A drawn app window (a made-up "MORF Studio", not a real product) tilts in 3D.
- The camera zooms to one part.
- A callout points at it, then hold.

**Library moves in it:** built in the piece. With a real product, give Claude real screenshots: they beat drawings.

**Files:** `source/index.html` (the canvas and layout), `source/shared/piece.css` (the look), `source/shared/piece.js` (the beats and motion).

**Sound:** Sound effects from HyperFrames' bundled set (copy them in, see [the examples README](../README.md)): click-soft.mp3, click.mp3, impact-bass-1.mp3, key-press.mp3, ping.mp3, whoosh-short.mp3, whoosh.mp3.
