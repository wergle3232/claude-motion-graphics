# Brand: MORF

A made-up brand, written 2026-10-08 for the motion graphics examples. MORF is a fictional AI platform for motion graphics; it is not a real company or product, and the examples must never present it as one. (A web check found no AI product called MORF; Morphic and MorphStudio exist in the same space, so keep the name spelled MORF.) Claude reads this file before every example piece. The colour and font tables are read by a script, so keep their layout.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #F3F1EC | paper white, the default video background |
| Surface | #E4E0D6 | slightly darker paper for panels and cards |
| Text | #0D0D0D | ink black: headlines, body, thick rules, solid black blocks |
| Muted text | #5E5A54 | labels, captions, secondary lines |
| Accent | #FF3D00 | blazing orange-red: the one colour that points at things |
| Second accent | #8E887E | warm grey: a second chart series, ghosts, shadows |
| Border | #0D0D0D | hard black rules and outlines, 4 to 8 px |

The orange-red on paper is about 3.3:1: use it for big display type, shapes and blocks, not for small text. Small text is ink or muted. Text on an orange-red block is ink, or paper when the block is big.

## Logo

- `logo.svg`: the wordmark, "MORF" in Archivo Black, the O replaced by a solid orange-red circle. Ink on paper.
- `logo-mark.svg`: the mark, a black square and an orange-red circle side by side (a square on its way to becoming a circle).
- The idea of the logo is the name: a square morphs into a circle. In motion, the mark's square becomes the circle that becomes the O.

## Look

Brutalist Swiss poster. Paper white, ink black, one blazing orange-red, nothing else. Huge heavy headlines set tight and cropped hard against the frame edge; a strict grid with thick black rules; flat colour, no gradients, no soft shadows; solid black or orange-red blocks behind words. Shapes are simple and geometric (square, circle, line, triangle) and do real work: they morph, slide, snap and stack. Motion is bold, fast and confident: hard cuts, snaps, morphs and squash on landing. For these examples, over the top is allowed (flash, shake, bounce) as long as it stays flat and graphic.

## Words

Short, blunt, upper or sentence case, no exclamation marks. Headlines can be single words. Labels in mono, uppercase, wide tracking ("02 / SHAPES").

## Avoid

Gradients, glows, soft drop shadows, purple, neon, rounded friendly UI, stock imagery, generic AI imagery (brains, robots, circuits), more than one accent colour in a frame, and anything that looks like a real AI company's branding.

## Sound

Punchy and percussive: hard hits, clicks, snaps and short whooshes on the moves; a driving electronic track for the showreel. Silent versions must still work.

## Our rules

Rules the user approved after a piece, one line each, with the piece they came from. Claude adds a line only when the user says yes.
