# Example: Shapes (MORF, 16:9, 7 s)

**The job:** Explaining an idea or a process quickly with simple shapes.

**A sentence to start one like it:** "Show "Idea, MORF, Motion" as an animated diagram made of simple shapes. Make the opening loud."

It was made over a short conversation, not from that one sentence: a first version, then a few notes. The sentence is a good way to start.

**What is on screen**

- 0 to 1.3 s: a storm of squares, circles and triangles pours in and is sucked into one black square.
- 1.5 to 2.85 s: the square snaps through circle, triangle, star and a red circle.
- 2.85 to 4 s: the camera dives into the red circle and pulls back out of the MORF mark in the diagram.
- 4 to 7 s: arrows draw, labels type on, a red pulse runs along the arrows, hold.

**Library moves in it:** `se-shape-storm`, `se-shape-morph`, and the [camera dive](../../library/recipes/camera-dive.md) recipe.

**Files:** `source/index.html` (the canvas and layout), `source/shared/piece.css` (the look), `source/shared/piece.js` (the beats and motion).

**Sound:** Sound effects from HyperFrames' bundled set (copy them in, see [the examples README](../README.md)): click-soft.mp3, click.mp3, impact-bass-1.mp3, key-press.mp3, ping.mp3, pop.mp3, whoosh-short.mp3, whoosh.mp3.
