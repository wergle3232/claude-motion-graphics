# Brand: Kiln

Written 2026-10-08 from answers in chat (a made-up brand for the plugin's trial pieces). Claude reads this file before every piece. Change it by hand or ask Claude to; the colour and font tables are read by a script, so keep their layout.

Kiln is booking and class software for small pottery studios: members book wheel time and classes from their phone, the studio sees who is coming and who has paid.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #F3EDE3 | warm clay-paper background |
| Surface | #FFFFFF | cards and phone screens |
| Text | #2B211C | headlines and body text, a dark fired-clay brown |
| Muted text | #6E625A | labels and captions |
| Accent | #C8553D | terracotta: the one colour that points |
| Second accent | #2F6B5E | glaze green: a second series, "booked" states |
| Border | #DDD2C2 | hairlines |

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weights | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | Fraunces | 600, 800 | Google Fonts |
| Body | Space Grotesk | 400, 600 | Google Fonts |
| Numbers and labels | IBM Plex Mono | 400, 600 | Google Fonts |

## Logo

- No logo file: the wordmark is "Kiln" set in Fraunces 800 with a terracotta dot after it ("Kiln.").

## Look

Warm, calm and tactile. Lots of paper-coloured space, one idea per frame, flat colour with soft shadows, rounded corners (16 px), a thin hairline grid. Motion is unhurried and precise: things settle into place like a pot set down, never bounce.

## Words

Plain, friendly, short. Sentence case. No exclamation marks. Talks to studio owners as "you".

## Avoid

Neon, glows, dark tech-startup looks, gradients, stock photos of hands on clay, centred text floating on empty colour.

## Sound

Gentle acoustic or lo-fi bed, about 90 BPM, no vocals; soft clicks and a chime for bookings. Silent versions must still work.
