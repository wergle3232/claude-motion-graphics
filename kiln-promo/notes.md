## What changed since version 1

- Round 1: hand-overs at 2.8 s, 5.8 s and 8.9 s overlapped old and new drawings into a muddle. Each scene now leaves before the next arrives; the green "Booked" button stays behind and flies into Saturday's slot.
- Round 1: effects peaked at -9 dB, too quiet. Raised to a -3 dB peak (effects only, no music, so loudness in LUFS is not the target).
- Round 2: the 16:9 end frame was empty on the right. The full week now returns beside the name.
- 9:16: headline larger (116 px) and the drawings lower, so the frame is less top-heavy.
